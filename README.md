# 🛍️ Retail Analytics: Walmart Business Intelligence Project Using Power BI

## 🎯 Purpose of the Project

This project aims to simulate a real-world **Business Intelligence solution** for a major retailer like Walmart. The objective is to extract actionable insights from raw sales data and present them through a compelling and interactive Power BI dashboard, supporting **data-driven decision-making** across sales, customer demographics, and branch performance.

---

## 📂 Dataset Used

**Filename:** `Walmart Sales Dataset.csv`

This dataset contains transactional-level sales records with the following key columns:
- `Invoice_ID`: Unique transaction identifier  
- `Branch`: Walmart store branch  
- `Age_Group`: Age category of customers  
- `Gender`: Customer gender  
- `City`: Location of the store  
- `Category`: Product category  
- `Invoice_Amount`: Sales amount for each invoice  
- `Date`: Date of transaction  
- `Payment_Method`: Method of payment used  
- `Customer_Rating`: Customer satisfaction rating (1–10 scale)

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The Power BI report is divided into **three analytical sections**, each focused on core areas of business performance:

### 1. 🧾 **Sales Analysis**
Delivers an overview of sales performance across time, product types, and customer demographics.
- **Sales by Age Group** – *Line Chart*
- **Year-wise Sales Trend** – *Filled Area Chart*
- **Sales by Product Category** – *Bar Chart*
- **Total Sales Amount** – *Card*
- **Average Customer Rating** – *Gauge Chart*

### 2. 👥 **Customer Analysis**
Focuses on understanding who the customers are and what they buy.
- **Sales Distribution by Age Group and Gender** – *Treemap*
- **Total Units Sold by Category** – *Pie Chart*
- **Customer Total by Gender** – *Pie Chart*

### 3. 🏬 **Branch Performance Analysis**
Highlights the top-performing branches across different KPIs.
- **Top 5 Branches by Sales Amount** – *Bar Chart*
- **Top 5 Branches by Customer Rating** – *Line Chart*
- **Top 5 Branches by Number of Sales** – *Line Chart*

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

- All three report pages include **interactive filters** for:
  - **Age Group**
  - **Year (with quarters)**
  - **Category**
  - **Gender**
  
- **Card visuals** used to clearly display KPIs like total sales amount
- **Gauge chart** effectively represents average customer satisfaction
- **Modern, intuitive layout** for seamless navigation and readability
- **PowerPoint presentation** created with live dashboard snapshots to support data storytelling for business stakeholders
